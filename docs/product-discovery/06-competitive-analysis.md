**Market expectation set by incumbents:** integrated recurring billing (card + direct debit) is the headline feature of every competitor above; belt/rank tracking is now table stakes; member apps, lead/CRM tools and SMS/email automation are common.

## 2. Athlete-side BJJ apps

Training journals and match-stat apps are a crowded, mostly single-developer App Store category: Post Black Belt (claims 6,000+ users), BJJ Journal: UnderHooked, Matlog (competition stats, match videos), BJJNote (sparring statistics), GrappleKit (video + journal), MatTime ("Strava for BJJ"), Rollbook, Marune ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1673061463), [Matlog](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/matlog/id6755608408), [BJJNote](https://mwm.ai/apps/bjjnote-bjj-training-journal/6746068256), [Rollbook blog — vendor](https://rollbook-web-86q.pages.dev/blog/best-bjj-training-apps-2026)). These are **not connected to the gym's management system**.

## 3. Where MatOS sits (facts vs hypotheses)

**Facts (from the code):**

- MatOS covers the core gym-management checklist (members, classes, attendance, belts with readiness, payments tracking, waivers, trials, kiosk, reports) **without** integrated recurring billing.
- It has three things the listed competitors were not reported to combine: a **natural-language assistant** that reads and (with confirmation) writes gym data; **structured match analysis** (positional intervals and events from local video, computed profiles, comparisons) linked to the same athlete record the gym manages; and **parent accounts + kiosk + kids-belt model** for kids programmes.
- It costs a gym nothing today; online payment uses the gym's own Stripe account.

**Hypotheses (need validation):**

- "Gym management + athlete performance analysis in one place" is an open position. The journal apps don't talk to the gym, and the management tools don't analyse matches.
- The assistant may be the most demo-able differentiator for owners who dislike admin, but it's unproven with real users and has a per-query cost.
- Without recurring billing, MatOS competes on price and simplicity, not completeness. Many owners won't switch without billing.

## 4. Opportunities observed

1. Billing done well and cheaply for small academies (Stripe/GoCardless with no platform fee) would remove the biggest objection.
2. A BJJ-native visual identity is rare; competitors present as generic fitness SaaS.
3. A free athlete app that grows into gyms ("your gym isn't on MatOS — invite your coach") is a bottom-up channel none of the listed management tools appear to use.
4. Kids-programme tooling (parents, waivers, kids belts, kiosk) matters to BJJ academies' revenue and is often an upsell elsewhere (not verified per competitor).
